AI Music Video & Face Swap Tools

A short guide to three browser-based tools that turn your own photos into music videos and runway clips with AI, no installs needed.

Burning Bridges AI

Burning Bridges AI lets you make a Burning Bridges music video with your own cast and the original soundtrack, using motion transfer, object swap and restyling tools.

Uptown Funk AI

Uptown Funk AI turns still photos into a personalized Uptown Funk music video, keeping the original choreography while swapping in your own cast.

Victoria Secret Runway

Victoria Secret Runway is an independent AI tool that turns a single portrait into a runway-style face-swap video. Not affiliated with Victoria's Secret.
